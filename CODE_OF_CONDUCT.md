
# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior include:
- Being respectful and kind.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Respecting different opinions.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions related to the project.

## Enforcement
Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All reports will be reviewed fairly.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Project maintainers may take appropriate action based on the severity of the behavior, including warnings or removal from the project community.
